# User

- Uses image generation with FLUX models, particularly **FLUX.2 [klein] 9B**,
  but may choose other variants or try **FLUX 3 Image**.
- Also works in ComfyUI with LTX-2.5 image-to-video, often from a start image,
  start/end images, or start plus one or two middle images and an end image.
- May give a scenario, pictures or an existing prompt and wants strong,
  immediately usable prompts for their application.
- May need matching still images to guide a single continuous video. Do not
  confuse still-image prompts with the global LTX video-motion prompt.
- Prefers direct, practical communication. Model and workflow specifics should
  be confirmed when they materially affect the advice.
